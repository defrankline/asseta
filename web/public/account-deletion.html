Delete your Asseta account and data

For users of the Asseta website, the Asseta Supervisor app and the Asseta Owner app, operated by Crysalion Tech Company Ltd.

Your own login (any user)

  1. Sign in at asseta.co.tz, or open the Asseta Supervisor or Asseta Owner app.
  2. Go to Delete my account: on the web under My access; in Asseta Supervisor in the account menu; in Asseta Owner under Settings.
  3. Confirm.

The login is removed at once, and the name, email and phone are wiped. Records you made for your company stay with the company, shown as "Former user", because they are the company's business records.

The last owner of a company cannot delete their own login; they close the company instead (below).

A whole company (company owner)

  1. On the web, go to Administration → Close company.
  2. Type the company name and a reason.
  3. You are offered a full export of your data first.

The company becomes read-only for 30 days, during which any owner can cancel. After 30 days all the company's data, files, logins and trackers are deleted permanently. Crysalion Tech Company Ltd keeps only its own invoices to the company and a short record that the company was closed.

Can't sign in?

Email frank.kachinga@gmail.com from the address on your account, with the company name. We verify the request with the company's owner and reply within 30 days.
