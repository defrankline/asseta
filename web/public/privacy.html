Privacy Policy

Asseta platform, the Asseta Supervisor app and the Asseta Owner app. Effective 12 November 2026.

In short: Asseta is a business system for equipment-hire companies. Your company decides what is recorded about its work; Crysalion Tech Company Ltd runs the platform for it. We do not sell data, show ads or track you across other apps. Data travels over HTTPS and is encrypted on the phone. You can delete your login yourself, or ask us to: see asseta.co.tz/account-deletion.

1. Who we are

Asseta (the website at asseta.co.tz and the Android and iOS apps "Asseta Supervisor" and "Asseta Owner") is operated by Crysalion Tech Company Ltd, Tanzania ("Crysalion", "we", "us").

Contact: frank.kachinga@gmail.com

2. Who controls your data

Asseta is used by companies that hire out equipment (each one a "company" or "tenant"). The company that gave you your login owns the business records kept in its Asseta account and decides why they are recorded: it is the controller. Crysalion operates the platform and processes those records on the company's behalf and on its instructions: it is the processor. For questions about what your company records about you, contact your company's administrator first; you can always contact us as well.

Crysalion is the controller only for the small amount of data it needs to run its own business with each company: the company's contact details, its subscription and our invoices to it.

3. Who can use the apps

The apps are for staff of companies that use Asseta. There is no public sign-up: logins are created by your company. The apps and the website are not intended for children and must not be used by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data about children.

5. How we use data

  • To provide Asseta to your company: record work, show it to the people your company allows, produce reports, statements and invoices.
  • To keep the system secure: sign-in, permissions, audit history, detecting misuse.
  • To support your company when it asks us for help.

We never sell data, never use it for advertising and never use one company's data for another company.

6. Where data is stored and who receives it

Records are stored on the Asseta server at asseta.co.tz, operated by Crysalion, with each company's data kept separate. Inside a company, people see only what their role and permissions allow. We use these service providers, only to run the service:

  • Our hosting provider, which runs the server for us.
  • Google ML Kit, built into the Supervisor app to read the hour-meter number from the photo on the phone. Google states that ML Kit sends it diagnostic and usage metrics (such as device model, app version, timing and error codes, with a per-installation identifier) over HTTPS and does not pass them to third parties.
  • Map tiles in the Owner app's fleet map are downloaded from OpenStreetMap's tile servers (or another tile provider your company configures). Those servers see the phone's IP address and which map area is shown, not your records.

We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it.

7. Security

  • All traffic between the apps, the website and the server uses HTTPS.
  • On the phone, the apps keep their data in an encrypted database (SQLCipher) and encrypted files, with keys held in the phone's secure keystore. Phone backups of app data are turned off.
  • Signing out, or being removed by your company, wipes the app's data from the phone.
  • Access on the server is limited by role and permission, and changes are recorded in an audit history.

8. How long we keep data

  • Business records are kept while the company uses Asseta, because they are the company's records.
  • When a person's login is deleted, the login is removed at once and the name, email and phone are wiped. Records that person made for the company stay with the company, shown as "Former user".
  • When a company closes its account, its data is deleted 30 days later, as described below. Crysalion keeps only its own invoices to the company and a short record that the company was closed.
  • Data on a phone is deleted when the user signs out or is removed.

9. Deleting your account and data

Full instructions: asseta.co.tz/account-deletion.

  • Your own login: sign in at asseta.co.tz, or open the Supervisor or Owner app, go to My account → Delete my account, and confirm. The login is removed at once, and your name, email and phone are wiped. Records you made for your company stay with the company, shown as "Former user", because they are the company's business records. The last owner of a company cannot delete their own login; they close the company instead.
  • A whole company (company owner): on the web, Administration → Data protection → Close company and delete its data. Type the company name and a reason. You are offered a full export of your data first. The company becomes read-only for 30 days, during which any owner can cancel. After 30 days all the company's data, files, logins and trackers are deleted permanently. Crysalion Tech Company Ltd keeps only its own invoices to the company and a short record that the company was closed.
  • Can't sign in? Email frank.kachinga@gmail.com from the address on your account, with the company name. We verify the request with the company's owner and reply within 30 days.

10. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete personal data about you. Because your company controls its business records, we will usually pass your request to your company's administrator and help them answer it. Contact us at frank.kachinga@gmail.com.
