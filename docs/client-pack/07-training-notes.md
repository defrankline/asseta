# Training notes

One short section per role. Each gives the main points to teach and where to read more in the [user manual](../user-manual/README.md).

**Everyone starts with:** the manual's opening page, for signing in, changing a password and the words Asseta uses.

**Teach this to everyone:**

- Never share a login or a phone.
- Recording something does not confirm it. A second person does that.
- You cannot decide your own request. That is the system working, not a fault.
- A flag or a difference is a question to answer, not an accusation.
- Records are never edited or deleted. Mistakes are corrected by a new, approved entry.

## Administrator

**Manual:** [Users and roles](../user-manual/01-tenant-owner.md#9-users-and-roles), and Tasks 3 and 10 of the Tenant Owner chapter.

## Supervisor

**Manual:** [Supervisor](../user-manual/03-supervisor.md)

Train in person, on the phone, outdoors, with the app in airplane mode for part of the session.

| Teach | Manual section |
|---|---|
| A full job day | A2 |
| Working offline, and reading the Sync status screen | A4 |
| Fuel and expenses | A5 and A6 |
| Collecting, transferring, acknowledging and handing over cash | A7 |
| Daily readings, baselines and corrections | A12 |
| Selling customer hours | A13 |
| Daily reports | A16 |
| Changeover between two customers in one day | A17 |

Stress these points:

- Take a new photo every time. Keep the date and time on automatic.
- Sync before leaving signal, and do not sign out with unsent records.
- If the phone says "Connect to refresh rules", get a signal and pull down to sync.
- Take a reading whenever the machine changes customer.
- Hand over all your cash unless a request to keep some has been approved.
- Tell the office at once if a phone is lost.

## Operator

**Manual:** [Operator](../user-manual/05-operator.md)

Operators usually only read notifications. An operator who holds cash has a balance recorded by a supervisor or manager, and should ask to see it. A company can also let an operator submit their own readings by giving them the additional role **Operator: submits own readings**; train them on sections A2 and A12 of the Supervisor chapter.

## Suggested order for the first two weeks

1. Owner and Manager: go-live checklist and policies.
2. Supervisors: a full supervised job day on a real machine, including one day offline.
3. Accountant: confirm that week's payments and remittances against the real statements.
4. Owner: watch the Accountability dashboard daily. Run Balance verification at the end of week one.
