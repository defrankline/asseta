**Full description** (1828/4000)

```
Asseta Supervisor is the field app for equipment-hire companies that run their business on Asseta. Site supervisors use it to record every job, hour and shilling where the work happens, so the owner can see what each machine earned and where the money went.

Your company gives you your login. There is no public sign-up: you need an account from a company that uses Asseta.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Jobs: see the jobs assigned to you, request new ones, start and complete them.
• Hour-meter readings: take a photo of the meter; the app reads the number on the phone and records the time and GPS position as evidence.
• Customer acknowledgement: the customer confirms the hours and signs on the screen.
• Fuel: record fuel issued to each machine.
• Expenses: record expenses with a photo of the receipt.
• Cash: record cash collected from customers and hand it over to the office, with a clear running balance.
• Customer hours sales: sell hours to a customer with a live total, split the payment between cash, bank and mobile money, and book the job together with the sale.
• Petty cash: request petty cash, see what was approved and account for it.
• Customers: add customers and keep their details up to date.
• Machines: see the machines you work with.

WORKS OFFLINE
Sites often have no signal. Everything you record is saved on the phone and sent to your company's Asseta server as soon as there is a connection. The Sync screen shows what is still waiting.

SECURE BY DESIGN
• Data on the phone is encrypted and wiped when you sign out.
• Camera and location are used only while you capture a record, never in the background.
• Everything travels over HTTPS to your company's Asseta account.
• No ads, no tracking, no data selling.

Asseta is operated by Crysalion Tech Company Ltd. Privacy policy: https://asseta.co.tz/privacy
```
