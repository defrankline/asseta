**Full description** (1909/4000)

```
Asseta Supervisor is the field app for equipment-hire companies that run their business on Asseta. Site supervisors use it to account for every machine hour and shilling where the work happens, so the owner can see what each machine earned and where the money went.

Your company gives you your login. There is no public sign-up: you need an account from a company that uses Asseta.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Sell hours: sell machine hours to a customer with a live total; split the payment between cash, bank, mobile money and credit.
• Machine report: take a photo of the hour meter (the app reads the number and records time and GPS), then account for every hour since the last reading: hours used by each customer and non-productive hours with a reason. Your manager approves it.
• Money: cash in hand, collections by payment method, hand cash over to the office, record expenses with a receipt photo, petty cash, and payments on customers' credit invoices.
• Dashboard: hours sold and used, productive and non-productive hours, your machines on a list or map with their report status.
• Fuel: record fuel issued to each machine, when your company uses it.
• Customers: add customers and keep their details up to date.

WORKS OFFLINE
Sites often have no signal. Everything you record is saved on the phone and sent to your company's Asseta server as soon as there is a connection. The Sync screen shows what is still waiting.

SECURE BY DESIGN
• Data on the phone is encrypted. Records not yet sent stay encrypted on the phone until they are sent.
• You can delete your account from the app (Settings, Delete my account).
• Camera and location are used only while you capture a record, never in the background.
• Everything travels over HTTPS to your company's Asseta account.
• No ads, no tracking, no data selling.

Asseta is operated by Crysalion Tech Company Ltd. Privacy policy: https://asseta.co.tz/privacy
```
