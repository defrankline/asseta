# App access (instructions for Google's reviewers)

Play Console → the app → Policy and programs → App content → App access.

Choose: **All or some functionality in my app is restricted.** Add one set of instructions per app.

**No password is ever written in this repository.** The owner types the demo login (username and password) directly
into the Console's App access form, which only Google's reviewers can see. Use dedicated demo accounts in the demo
company (Wambura & Sons Ltd demo tenant on asseta.co.tz), never a real customer's login, and keep them active for as
long as the apps are under review or published (reviews can recur at any update).

| Username / email | *(owner enters the demo supervisor login in the Console)* |
| Password | *(owner enters it in the Console)* |
| Any other information required to access your app | text below |

```
Asseta Supervisor is a business app for staff of equipment-hire companies. Accounts are created by the company; there is no public sign-up.
1. Open the app and tap "Sign in". The company sign-in page (asseta.co.tz) opens in a browser tab.

The Jobs tab lists demo jobs. Money shows petty cash, customers and cash. Camera and location are asked for only when you capture a meter reading or a receipt; you can decline location and continue. Please do not delete the demo account (My account > Delete my account) because other reviews use it.

```
