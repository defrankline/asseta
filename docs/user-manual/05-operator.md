# Asseta user manual: Operator

> Part of the [Asseta user manual](README.md). Read the README first for signing in and the glossary.

## Who this is for

This manual is for machine operators: the people who drive and operate the excavators, loaders, graders and other equipment.

In Asseta, an operator is mainly a **record**, not a daily user:

- The office registers you as a **staff member** of kind *Operator* (**Operations → Staff**).
- The office assigns you to machines with start and end dates (on the machine's **Staff assignments** tab).
- Your name then appears on the fleet map and in the machine details as the machine's operator.

You do **not** need to record readings, jobs, fuel or cash yourself. Your **supervisor** does that in the Asseta Supervisor app or the web console. There is no separate operator mobile app.

Your company may also give you an Asseta login with the **Operator** role. This manual explains what that login lets you do.

## What you can do

With an Operator login you can:

- Sign in to the web console at <https://asseta.co.tz>.
- Read your **notifications**.
- Manage your own **sign-in and security**: change your password and see your access.

You cannot see jobs, machines, money or reports. If you need any of these, ask your supervisor or the owner.

### If you hold company cash

You may collect cash from customers, or receive cash from a colleague, even without an Asseta login. If your company uses the cash accountability controls, Asseta still keeps your own record as a **cash custodian**:

- Your supervisor or manager records each collection, transfer and handover **on your behalf**, under your name.
- When you hand cash to someone, they record the transfer and the **amount they actually received**. Until then the cash is still counted as yours.
- When cash reaches the office, the office counts it and records the amount received. Only then does your balance go down.
- If you need to keep some cash (for example for a working float), ask your supervisor to send a **request to keep cash** for approval. Without an approved request, the whole balance is expected to be handed over.
- Your company may set **how many days cash may be held** before it is handed over (it can be longer at remote sites). Cash held longer is flagged **Held longer than allowed** for the office to follow up.
- When cash is paid into the company's bank account or mobile-money wallet, keep the **deposit slip or payment message**. Your company may require its transaction reference and a photo of it.

Ask for a receipt or check with your supervisor that every amount was recorded. A difference between what was declared and what was counted is reviewed by the office; it is a check, not an accusation.

If your company uses the **people scorecard**, the office may see, per person, items to look at on the machines and cash they were responsible for, such as a missing daily report or a cash difference. It is a list of questions to check, not a verdict.

## Where you work

| Place | What you'll see |
|---|---|
| Web console, **Administration → Notifications** | Your notification inbox |
| Web console, **My access** | Your company, your roles, and **Sign-in & security** (change password) |

## Tasks

### Sign in for the first time

1. Open <https://asseta.co.tz> in a web browser.
2. Click to sign in. The secure Asseta sign-in page opens.
3. Enter the username and temporary password your company gave you.
4. If asked, choose a new password and confirm it.

   *What you'll see:* the Asseta console with only a few menus. That is expected for this role.

### Read your notifications

1. Open **Notifications** in the side menu.
2. Click a notification to read it. You can mark notifications as read.

### Change your password

1. Open **My access**.
2. Under **Sign-in & security**, choose to change your password. The secure Asseta sign-in page opens.
3. Enter your current password and the new one, then confirm.

### Sign out

Click the **Sign out** icon in the top bar. Always sign out on a shared computer.

## Good practice

- Do not share your login. Each person has their own.
- Tell your supervisor about breakdowns, standby and downtime as they happen, so they can log them on the job. This is how the customer is billed fairly.
- Tell your supervisor when the machine is **moved** to or from a site, so the movement is logged. If your company uses revenue checks, movement counts as customer work until it is explained and approved.
- Run the engine only for work. Your company may review days when an engine runs without a job, and fuel used against engine hours.
- Make sure your supervisor takes the hour-meter photo before you start and after you finish a job.
- If your company asks for daily readings, make sure the meter is read every working day, including days without a job.
- Hand over company cash promptly and only to the person your company names, and check it is recorded.

## Troubleshooting and FAQ

**I only see Notifications and My access.**
That is correct for the Operator role.

**My name is wrong on the fleet map, or I am shown on the wrong machine.**
Ask the owner or manager to correct your staff record or your machine assignment.

**I forgot my password.**
Ask your company's administrator (normally the owner) to reset it.

**I want to record work myself.**
Field records are made by supervisors. If your company wants you to do this, the owner can give your login the Supervisor role.
