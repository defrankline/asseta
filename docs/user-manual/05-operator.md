# Asseta user manual: Operator

> Part of the [Asseta user manual](README.md). Read the README first for signing in and the glossary.

## Who this is for

This manual is for machine operators: the people who drive and operate the excavators, loaders, graders and other equipment.

In Asseta Release 1, an operator is mainly a **record**, not a daily user:

- The office registers you as a **staff member** of kind *Operator* (**Operations → Staff**).
- The office assigns you to machines with start and end dates (on the machine's **Staff assignments** tab).
- Your name then appears on the fleet map and in the machine details as the machine's operator.

You do **not** need to record readings, jobs, fuel or cash yourself. Your **supervisor** does that in the Asseta Supervisor app. There is no separate operator mobile app.

Your company may also give you an Asseta login with the **Operator** role. This manual explains what that login lets you do.

## What you can do

With an Operator login you can:

- Sign in to the web console at <https://asseta.co.tz>.
- Read your **notifications**.
- Manage your own **sign-in and security**: change your password and see your access.

You cannot see jobs, machines, money or reports. If you need any of these, ask your supervisor or the owner.

## Where you work

| Place | What you'll see |
|---|---|
| Web console, **Administration → Notifications** | Your notification inbox |
| Web console, **My access** | Your company, your roles, and **Sign-in & security** (change password) |

## Tasks

### Sign in for the first time

1. Open <https://asseta.co.tz> in a web browser.
2. Click to sign in. The secure Asseta sign-in page opens.
3. Enter the username and temporary password your company gave you.
4. If asked, choose a new password and confirm it.

   *What you'll see:* the Asseta console with only a few menus. That is expected for this role.

### Read your notifications

1. Open **Notifications** in the side menu.
2. Click a notification to read it. You can mark notifications as read.

### Change your password

1. Open **My access**.
2. Under **Sign-in & security**, choose to change your password. The secure Asseta sign-in page opens.
3. Enter your current password and the new one, then confirm.

### Sign out

Click the **Sign out** icon in the top bar. Always sign out on a shared computer.

## Good practice

- Do not share your login. Each person has their own.
- Tell your supervisor about breakdowns, standby and downtime as they happen, so they can log them on the job. This is how the customer is billed fairly.
- Make sure your supervisor takes the hour-meter photo before you start and after you finish a job.

## Troubleshooting and FAQ

**I only see Notifications and My access.**
That is correct for the Operator role.

**My name is wrong on the fleet map, or I am shown on the wrong machine.**
Ask the owner or manager to correct your staff record or your machine assignment.

**I forgot my password.**
Ask your company's administrator (normally the owner) to reset it.

**I want to record work myself.**
In Release 1, field records are made by supervisors. If your company wants you to do this, the owner can give your login the Supervisor role.
